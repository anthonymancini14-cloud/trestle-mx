# Electrical safety for maintenance planning

Reference: NFPA 70E, Standard for Electrical Safety in the Workplace (concepts paraphrased). Site electrical safety program governs.

- **Establish an electrically safe work condition** before work on or near exposed conductors: identify all sources from current drawings, open the disconnect, visually verify the break where possible, apply locks and tags, test for absence of voltage on each phase to ground and phase to phase, with the meter verified on a known source before and after.
- **Until absence of voltage is proven, it is energized work.** Plan the PPE and boundaries from the equipment's arc flash label: arc flash boundary, limited and restricted approach boundaries, required arc rating.
- **Energized work** (including diagnostics and infrared scans under load) needs justification and, where required by the program, an energized work permit. Thermography is commonly done energized with the cover removed by a qualified person using label PPE.
- **Torque and terminations:** use manufacturer torque values; a hot connection found by infrared usually means a loose or degraded lug. Replace damaged lugs rather than re-torquing over damage.
- **Motors and drives:** a VFD's DC bus holds charge after disconnect; wait the posted time and measure. Megger a motor that has been wet or has tripped on ground fault before re-energizing.
- **Qualified persons only** for anything inside a boundary. Mechanical crafts working on driven equipment rely on the electrical isolation, not their own judgment of it.
