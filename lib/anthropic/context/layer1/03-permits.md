# Permit-required work

Paraphrased from OSHA 29 CFR 1910.146 (permit-required confined spaces) and 1910.252 (welding, cutting and brazing). Site permit programs govern.

## Confined space entry

A space large enough to enter, with limited entry/exit, not designed for occupancy: tanks, pits, vaults, wet wells, boiler drums, large pump casings and ducts. If it also has a hazard (atmosphere, engulfment, configuration, or other serious hazard) it is permit-required.

Plan for: the entry permit, isolation of every line into the space (blinds or double block and bleed, not a single closed valve), atmospheric testing before and during entry (oxygen, flammables, toxics such as hydrogen sulfide or chlorine), ventilation, an attendant, and a rescue plan that is actually available on the day.

## Hot work

Welding, cutting, grinding and torch work outside a designated area. Plan for: the hot work permit, a fire watch during and after the work, removing or shielding combustibles, gas testing where flammables may be present, and fire extinguishers on hand.

## Other common permits and procedures

- **Line break / opening process equipment** for hazardous fluids (chemicals, steam, high pressure).
- **Crane and rigging lift plans** for critical or heavy lifts; know the weight before you plan the hoist.
- **Working at heights** (fan decks, elevated platforms) with fall protection and anchor points.
- **Excavation** near buried utilities.
